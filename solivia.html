Infection Control

Why Infection Control is a critical clinical priority.

Hospital-acquired infections (HAIs) are among the most preventable causes of patient harm globally. The Solivia platform addresses this through clinically validated, evidence-based infection control devices endorsed by the world's leading health authorities.

~7%
HAI Rate in Hospitals
On average, 1 in 14 patients in acute care hospitals acquires a healthcare-associated infection, making IC one of the highest-impact areas of patient safety intervention.
40–60%
SSI Reduction with CHG
Pre-operative CHG skin preparation is proven to significantly reduce Surgical Site Infections, endorsed by CDC, WHO and AORN in their clinical practice guidelines worldwide.
96 hrs
Sustained Antimicrobial Action
Our CHG formulations provide up to 96 hours of sustained microbial suppression, effective against gram-positive, gram-negative bacteria, yeasts and MRSA.

How the Solivia platform addresses HAIs across the care continuum

Before Surgery
Antisepsis & Prep

CHG Applicators for pre-operative skin antisepsis and Surgical Scrub Brushes for hand and forearm prep, eliminating microbial load before the first incision.

During Surgery
Sterile Barrier Maintenance

Certified sterile drapes, gowns and equipment covers maintaining the surgical sterile field, with low-lint construction protecting from airborne contamination.

After Surgery
Post-Procedure Protection

Disinfectant Caps for IV access points, ANTT-packed dressing kits and OR Turnaround Kits for clean, rapid room resets, closing the HAI prevention loop.

Infection Control Medical Devices
CHG Antisepsis

CHG Applicators & Swab Sticks

No-touch CHG + IPA applicator system combining 2% Chlorhexidine Gluconate and 70% Isopropyl Alcohol. Kills microbes within 30 seconds and provides up to 96 hours of sustained antimicrobial protection. Endorsed by CDC, WHO, AORN and ACS. Available in 5.5 mL, 10.5 mL and 26 mL sizes, in clear or tinted solutions.

✓ ISO 17025-accredited testing · 3-year shelf life · India's first CHG Applicator
Hand Antisepsis

Surgical Scrub Brushes

Dual-action preoperative scrub brushes: a soft foam sponge paired with gentle bristles for thorough hand and forearm antisepsis. Latex-free, biocompatible and colour-coded by formulation: 7.5% Povidone-Iodine, 4% CHG, 2% CHG and Dry Brush. The sponge absorbs up to 50 mL of antiseptic and a fingernail cleaner is included.

✓ ISO 13485 & GMP certified · 2-year shelf life · WHO/CDC compliant

Passive disinfection caps for IV access devices and needle-free connectors, pre-saturated with 70% IPA, providing up to 7 days of continuous IV access protection. PVC-, latex- and DEHP-free. Compatible with all needle-free valves. Reduces CRBSIs and saves nursing time at the point of care.

✓ 7-day protection · Needle-free compatible · Reduces CRBSIs

Meet the Founder
Deepika Gulati
Founder & CEO · Solivia Pte. Ltd. · Singapore

Deepika Gulati is a Singapore citizen, technology and business leader who founded Solivia Pte. Ltd. to make a profound impact globally. Meaning "the path lit by sunlight," Solivia was built with a clear mission: to make certified, safe and high-quality medical products accessible across advanced, emerging and underserved healthcare markets worldwide.

Deepika holds a Master's degree in Psychology and began her professional journey as an intern with the American Red Cross in Delhi, working directly with underprivileged communities. This early experience deeply shaped her belief in service, dignity and social impact, strengthening a lifelong commitment to improving access to care for those who need it most.

Her unique perspective on global clinical supply chains draws from years of experience with medical industry leaders including Abbott and ResMed. This expertise is further backed by her family's 30-plus-year legacy in medical device manufacturing in India, giving her exceptional exposure to quality, rigorous compliance, absolute reliability and the patient-focused mindset that global healthcare demands.

Through Solivia, Deepika brings together her background in psychology, healthcare, technology, manufacturing and community service to build a trusted global clinical distribution platform that empowers hospitals, clinics, distributors, governments and care providers worldwide.
